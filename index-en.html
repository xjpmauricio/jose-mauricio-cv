About Me

Personal background and motivation.

My path in IT has been far from conventional. My foray into music might initially seem irrelevant for a résumé, but it has enriched me in many ways: teamwork, persistence, determination, and more. Music gave me cultural depth and shaped me into an open-minded person, receptive to new and unfamiliar experiences.

From an early age, I showed a strong interest in computers. I remember receiving a Timex 2068 in the mid-1980s, the American version of the famous ZX Spectrum. This sparked a lifelong fascination with these machines. I started programming in BASIC and later progressed to machine code. This passion lasted several years until music took over when I was given a guitar. That began another of my passions: Music.

I studied music at the Conservatório de Setúbal, focusing on Classical Guitar. A love for electric guitar later drew me toward Jazz, Funk, and Soul. I became a professional musician, recorded several works, and performed internationally. While financial stability was limited in Portugal’s music scene, my passion kept me going until personal circumstances led me back to IT. This time, someone gifted me a PC, reigniting my love for computers, now with the Internet opening new worlds of opportunity.

Since then, I haven’t stopped. Programming has become both a personal passion and a source of income, giving me the ideal professional situation: doing work I truly love.

Beyond IT, I revisited another passion: photography. After a hiatus of over ten years, I returned to photography during the pandemic as a way to find meaning in isolation. I have developed several photographic projects exploring my vision of Setúbal and its surroundings. Over the years, I’ve participated in exhibitions, sharing my work with the public and receiving recognition for my artistic approach, with some photos published in National Geographic. My portfolio can be viewed at facebook.com/josemauriciophotography
